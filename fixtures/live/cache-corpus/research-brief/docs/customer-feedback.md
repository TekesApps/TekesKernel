# Customer feedback, September 2026

Collected from support tickets and three customer calls.

- Three customers reported that exporting to PDF takes several minutes for
  files larger than 50 MB. One of them called it "the reason we still keep the
  old tool around".
- Two support tickets say that setting up SSO is confusing for admins: the
  identity-provider metadata field accepts a URL but the help text asks for an
  XML file.
- Several users praised the new comment threads and the faster search.
- One customer asked for a dark mode; this is already on the roadmap for Q1.
- A design agency asked whether the Team plan will keep its current price. It
  will (see the 30 September sync).
